# Figure provenance

## Model illustrations

`figures/model_3d.png` and `figures/model_side.png` are retained companion-project illustrations. Their exact original snapshot/render recipe was not recovered. They are schematic context, not measured geometry or evidence of deformation mechanisms. Publication/reuse permissions are not established by this numerical audit.

## Figures 2–3: rebuilt from final configurations

`render_audited_views.py` generates the six `scratch_morph_*.png` and six `scratch_strain_*.pdf` panels from the reduced position/type/identifier dumps in `evidence/visualization/`. It does not crop legacy renders. The shared colorbar is `scratch_strain_cbar.pdf`. The same relaxed Realization 1 workpiece is the reference for every case; it precedes scratch equilibration, not just translation. Positions are unchanged and the reference uses the scratch-cell vacuum to avoid artificial periodic top–bottom neighbors.

All are outside the quantitative 4–16 nm window; they are not a matched-travel comparison. No dynamic clearance, contact-area, detachment, periodic shear-band, or window-specific mechanism is inferred from them.

`data/visualization_manifest.json` supplies separate source/output paths, original and reduced SHA-256 hashes, clocks, reference definition, atom counts, and strain checks for each pair. Original `final.data` header steps were read directly. The tool centroid is the mean of the discrete carbon positions, which can differ slightly from the mathematical sphere center. All 543778 workpiece IDs and species match the reference in each case. Carbon is excluded before strain evaluation. Invalid/nonfinite strain is excluded from statistics and colored gray, never silently assigned zero. Cutoff: 3.375 Å. Slice: y=78 Å, thickness 30 Å. Shared strain range: 0–1, with values above 1 saturated. Scale bars use the known orthographic field of view.

### Morphology camera

The six morphology panels use neutral gray for all species, shadows and ambient occlusion, and one fixed orthographic camera looking from above (+z). The direction is the normalized vector (-0.45, 0.65, -0.61), z-up, target (324, 78, 111) Å, half-height field of view 140 Å, and 1200 × 1000 pixels. The same display selection retains 180 ≤ x ≤ 468 Å and z ≥ 85 Å in every case. The lower cut face is artificial, not a physical free surface. Coordinates are unchanged and there is no vertical exaggeration. The arrow is the camera projection of minus x; the 5 nm scale bar uses 1000 / 280 pixels per Å. No groove or pile-up annotation is assigned without a separate geometric identification. Per-case settings are in `morphology_camera`; strain analysis is unchanged.

### Superseded images

The former A25B10 strain image was from step 13088 relative to 6544, both before translation at 161080. It was not an endpoint. Other former captions also mismatched their raw render manifests. ## Quantitative figures

`rebuild_analysis.py --offline` regenerates `glass_structure*.pdf` from packaged RDF data, `baseline_blocks.pdf`, `phase_response*.pdf`, and `force_power*.pdf` from packaged force data. Refer to filenames rather than historical figure numbers. Model illustrations are retained assets, not regenerated by this script.

## Coordinates and phase

`data/run_provenance.csv` documents full-precision phase offsets against absolute LAMMPS Time, physical translation origins, separate force/dump strides, and restart segment counts. `data/restart_segments.json` gives segment bounds and the analysis uses later records in overlapping restart intervals. Baseline logged x has a +4.8 Å offset because its reporting variable uses 20 ps whereas fix move begins at 4 ps. Relative-travel differences cancel this offset. It is not applied to the atomistic configurations.
